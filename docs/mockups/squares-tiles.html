MISSION 1

What is a square?

Picture tiles on a floor. Lay them out 3 rows of 3, and you have a square made of 9 tiles. That is why 9 is called the “square” of 3.

ENIAC can make a whole list of squares, and you will wire it up. No typing. In 1946, programming meant plugging in cables!

Let’s go
9+3+3+1=16
↻ Again
STEP 0

Here is the trick

To make the square one size bigger, add a row, add a column, and add 1 tile in the corner.

ENIAC never multiplies here. It just adds, and adding is what it did best.
