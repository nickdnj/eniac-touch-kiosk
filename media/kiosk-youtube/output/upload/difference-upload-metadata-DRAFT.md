You wire a few cables and switches, press init, and ENIAC builds the table the way Charles Babbage designed his Difference Engine to.

The kiosk runs Brian L. Stuart's ENIAC simulator, and this mission uses his program from the tutorial "Polynomial Tables on the ENIAC". Simulator: https://github.com/blstuart/eniac-simulator
