## Music cue sheet (ducked bed)

**Direction (Gate 1 decision 5):** a **1940s-period bed** with a big-band and swing-era feel. Keep it light: brushes, upright bass, muted brass, clarinet or sax section, and **no vocals**. Duck it under the narration throughout. It should evoke the era ENIAC was born in without fighting the voice. Use one track, or two from the same album for continuity. Levels are relative to the bed's full level.

> [!warning] Real 1940s commercial recordings are *not* public domain yet
> Under the Music Modernization Act, US sound recordings published 1923–1946 are protected for 100 years. **Recordings published in 1940 enter the PD on 1 Jan 2041, and 1946 recordings on 1 Jan 2047.** As of 2026, only recordings published **before 1926** are PD (1925 entered on 1 Jan 2026). So a genuine 1940s Glenn Miller or Benny Goodman record is not usable even when the tune itself is old.
>
> The only truly PD "period" audio today is pre-1926 dance-band and jazz recordings, for example the Library of Congress National Jukebox or Citizen DJ public-domain sets. Those sound 1920s, not 1940s, so treat them as a fallback only.
>
> **Recommendation:** candidate 1, then 2.
