# Edit decision list: Program ENIAC: Our New Touch-Screen Exhibit (v3)

**v3, 2026-10-05.** This is a re-cut against the re-recorded kiosk footage in `assets/video/source-v3/`. The kiosk UI changed, so every in and out point was re-derived. The structure, chapters and editorial rules are unchanged from the approved v2 build (`scripts/assemble_v2.py`). The v2 EDL is kept as `edit-decision-list.v2.md`.

The machine-readable twin is `storyboard-data.json` (v3), generated by `scripts/gen_storyboard_v3.py`. Edit the generator, not the JSON.

## How the cut points were verified

1. **Line positions.** I cross-correlated all 134 per-line WAVs in `assets/audio/source-lines-v3/` into their v3 MP4s (8 kHz, normalized, energy floor). Every line was found with a score of 0.996 to 1.000. The results are in `output/build/v3-line-positions.json`. The `*-timeline.json` offsets were not trusted: the trajectory and primes files carry hand cuts (`mux-cuts.txt`).
2. **Silence.** I ran `silencedetect=noise=-40dB:d=0.25` on every v3 MP4. Every *audible* in or out point below sits inside a detected silence; the gap is shown. Muted footage (J, K, O) only needs a clean visual.
3. **Visual.** I used `scdet` scene changes, then 25 fps frame steps at every edge. Burned-in captions were checked line by line where they mattered (G in, E in).
4. **Bridge WAVs.** `tour-27.wav` and `finale-00.wav` in `source-lines-v3/` are byte-identical to v2. The tour-27 trim at 2.21 still sits in its word gap (2.06–2.37).

**Timecodes.** Source times are seconds in the v3 file. Program times are the main program, excluding the 8.12 s VCF intro; add 8.12 for the final file. Units crossfade (video only) by 0.4 s, or 0.5 s after A, NO and P, exactly as in v2. That's why a unit's program out time overlaps the next unit's in.
