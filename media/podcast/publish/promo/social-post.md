## Option A — leads with the correction (recommended)

ENIAC's 18,000 tubes didn't work because they found better tubes. They
worked because the circuits used tubes only as on/off switches, with
signals regenerated at every stage — a weak tube that still switched was
a working tube. That's in the machine's own 1946 documentation. Part 1 of
a 4-part series, sourced to the original reports. [link]
