# Fact-check: "ENIAC Plays Battleship" (recut v3.1, after draft 1)

**Date:** 2026-10-23. **v3 recut:** after Nick watched draft 1, every line and overlay was re-audited for one kind of overclaim: saying or implying that the kiosk runs, or the viewer plays on, the real 1946 machine. **The kiosk runs Brian L. Stuart's pulse-level simulation of ENIAC (D1).** Rows superseded by v3 are marked **Superseded** and point to the new rows V36 to V46. **v3.1 (Nick, 2026-10-23):** the programmers segment is cut (VO05, S6, the P3 photo, the R6 honor roll). Rows V9 to V11 are marked **Cut**; they stay here as checked facts for the museum's own materials. **Covers:** every spoken line in `storyboard/script.md` (VO01 to VO16), every on-screen overlay in `storyboard/storyboard.md`, and the kiosk screen text that will be visible in the footage.
**Fact base (read only, not edited):** `~/Workspaces/eniac-kiosk-youtube/research/fact-check.md` (row ids A*, B*, D*, E* below), `research/trajectory-history.md` (TH §), `research/video-briefing-2026-10-12.md` (M2, M2b), `research/pronunciation-aliases.json`; this repo's `research/kiosk-gunnery-battleship-mission.md` (KGM); the mission file `missions/battleship.yaml` on kiosk branch `gunnery-game` @ `56274b7` (BSY, read only).

**Verdicts:** **Correct** · **Nuance** (true as worded here; the row says what the wording avoids) · **Game** (a rule or number of the game, not a history claim; source is the kiosk) · **Measured** (a number the real core produced through the kiosk bridge, repeatable) · **Blocked** (true only after a precondition) · **Excluded** (deliberately not in the script).
