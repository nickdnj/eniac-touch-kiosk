## How the cut points were verified (v4)

1. **Line positions.** All 102 lines of the five re-recorded sources (`assets/audio/source-lines-v3/` WAVs, unchanged) were cross-correlated into the v4 MP4s. Every line was found with a score of 0.996 or better; the results are in `output/build/v4-line-positions.json`.
   - The drift is **not a constant offset**. The tour goes from +0.0 s at the start to about +1.6 s after the Explore section. Squares is unchanged until about 105 s, then gains about 1 s in each lamp-play pause, reaching +2.1 s.
   - So every edge was re-found from scratch, not shifted.
2. **Silence.** `silencedetect=noise=-40dB:d=0.25` was run on every v4 MP4. Every audible edge sits inside a gap, listed per row.
3. **Visual.** `scdet` scene changes, plus 25 fps frame steps at each edge.
4. **Overlay clear space** was re-measured on unlabelled full-res v4 frames and is unchanged from v3:
   - Explore header x 588 to 1178;
   - Primes header x 640 to 1193;
   - Trajectory header x 693 to 1193;
   - Trajectory right column x 1420 to 1890, y 425 to 680.

   **So all v3 overlay positions stand, and no overlay change is needed.**

Program times exclude the 8.12 s VCF intro; the "Final" column includes it. Units crossfade (video only) by 0.4 s, or 0.5 s after A, NO and P, as before.
