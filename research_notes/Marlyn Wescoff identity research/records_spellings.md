# Marlyn Wescoff Meltzer: records, spellings, and "Marlene Wescott"

## (a) Vital facts, family, spouse

### Takeaway
Find a Grave transcribes her 2008 obituary and her husband's 1997 obituary. Philip Meltzer (1918-1997) is described as a dentist and Army captain, graduated Temple dental school 1948, first name "Phil". This matches Mauchly's "a dentist by the name of Phil" and is strong evidence that he means her.

### Cited Findings
- Born 2 Mar 1922, Philadelphia; died Thursday 7 Dec 2008 (memorial header says 4 Dec; the obituary text says 7 Dec, and Wikipedia agrees), Yardley, Bucks Co., PA; buried Fountain Lawn Memorial Park, Ewing Twp, NJ — [Find a Grave, Marlyn Wescoff Meltzer](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/211908992/marlyn-meltzer) (user-contributed transcription of obituary; medium-high)
- Obituary: Temple University graduate; worked at Moore School five years; chosen by Army in 1945 as one of first six ENIAC programmers; lived in Yardley 51 years — same Find a Grave page
- Survivors: son Hugh Meltzer; daughter Joy (and Craig Levine); grandchildren Aaron Webb-Meltzer, Aaron Levine, Sarah Levine — same page
- Spouse: Dr. Philip Meltzer, 16 Oct 1918 - 10 Nov 1997, Middletown, PA — [Find a Grave, Philip Meltzer](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/280641824) (transcribed obituary; medium-high)
- His obituary calls him "a dentist"; Temple dental school graduate 1948; US Army captain; member of American, NJ and Mercer County dental associations; parents Hyamm and Lena Meltzer; sister Marcy Caster — same page. Note: memorial lists age 79 but obituary says 69; unresolved (a 1918 birth makes 79 correct).
- Married Philip Meltzer 2 Feb 1947, Philadelphia, citing a county marriage record (via FamilySearch), per a search-result summary of [WikiTree Wescoff-1](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Wescoff-1) (page itself did not load for me; second-hand, medium-low)
- Resigned from ENIAC team in 1947 "to get married" — [Wikipedia, Marlyn Meltzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlyn_Meltzer); [ETHW](https://ethw.org/Marlyn_Meltzer)
- Spanish-language profile names "Dr. Philip Meltzer" — [EcuRed](https://www.ecured.cu/Marlyn_Wescoff_Meltzer) (low; derivative)
- Temple University, graduated 1942, social studies degree (per Penn Almanac) — [Penn Almanac](https://almanac.upenn.edu/articles/75th-anniversary-of-the-electronic-numerical-integrator-and-computer-eniac) (high, but reported via search summary)

### Inferences
- Mauchly's "history major" and "trained as a high-school history teacher" are consistent with a Temple social-studies/education degree, but no source I read says "history" for her.
- Marriage in Feb 1947 followed by dental school in 1948 means in the early 1940s Phil was not yet a dentist; Mauchly's remark describes him as of 1973 recollection.

### Gaps
- Parents' names and siblings of Marlyn: not found (not in Wikipedia, ETHW, Find a Grave summaries).
- Did not obtain primary records (FamilySearch, census 1930-1950, SSDI, Inquirer obituary, Templar yearbook); none accessible via the tools used.

## (b) Is there a real "Marlene Wescott" at Penn/Moore School?

### Takeaway
Found no record of any such person. Every source on the Moore School/ENIAC women uses Marlyn Wescoff; "Marlene Wescott" appears only in Mauchly's transcript.

### Cited Findings
- Transcript wording: "Another one, Marlene Wescott, who married a dentist by the name of Phil--can't remember his last name" — [Smithsonian NMAH AC0196 mauc730110 PDF](https://www.si.edu/media/NMAH/NMAH-AC0196_mauc730110.pdf), text extracted locally (line ~3542 of pdftotext output). It comes in a passage listing women hired as computers, immediately before he turns to Holberton and the group of "6 girls" for ENIAC.
- Mauchly says he hired her "as one of my computers on this Signal Corps project" and that she "didn't have to know any mathematics" — same source.
- A web search for "Marlene Wescott" with ENIAC/Mauchly/Moore School returned nothing naming such a person — [Penn Engineering ENIAC history](https://www.engineering.upenn.edu/about/history-heritage/eniac/) lists Marlyn Wescoff Meltzer among the six.
- Penn Almanac: she was brought to the Moore School for weather calculations because she could run an adding machine, then moved to ballistics — [Penn Almanac](https://almanac.upenn.edu/articles/75th-anniversary-of-the-electronic-numerical-integrator-and-computer-eniac) (via search summary).

### Inferences
- Same given-name-with-dentist-Phil, Temple background, and Moore School computer role point to one person; a second woman with these features is not evidenced.

### Gaps
- No Moore School personnel lists, BRL rosters, or 1946 press materials checked; absence is only "not found in searches".
- Not verified that Meltzer ever worked on the Signal Corps radar-antenna project specifically.

## (c) Spellings and transcription errors

### Takeaway
"Wescott" is a rare variant for her; "Marlene" not found for her anywhere. Mauchly's own transcript shows he was misremembering/mis-transcribing names.

### Cited Findings
- Dominant spelling "Marlyn Wescoff" / "Marlyn Wescoff Meltzer" across Wikipedia, ETHW, Find a Grave, WikiTree, EcuRed (links above).
- Variant "Marilyn Wescott Meltzer" is the title of the Latin Wikipedia article, per [English Wikipedia fetch result](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlyn_Meltzer) (low; I did not open the Latin page).
- The PDF contains no transcriber's note or errata I could find; grep for "errat" returned nothing, and the only "transcri" hit (line 1158) is about shorthand. Transcriber name not shown in the text. Note the same transcript speaks of "Friden desk calculator" after a self-correction from "Marchant", showing verbatim spoken text.
- Mauchly himself says he can't remember Phil's last name and "I don't remember what school" — same PDF.

### Inferences
- Transcription from tape of "Wescoff" to "Wescott" and "Marlyn" to "Marlene" is plausible (f/t, final vowels), but I found no study of this specific collection; this is inference only.

### Gaps
- No other "Westcott"/"Wescott" spelling for her in 1940s documents found.
- Did not examine whether the Smithsonian collection has other mis-spelled names (not searched).
- Candidate-other-woman search (Wescott/Westcott at Moore School 1941-46): nothing found, but sources searched were limited.
